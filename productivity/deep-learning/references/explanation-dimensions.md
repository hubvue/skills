# Explanation Dimensions

Whenever possible, cover these dimensions.

## A. Responsibility
- What is this part responsible for?
- What is outside its scope?

## B. Position
- Where is it located in the larger system?
- What depends on it upstream and downstream?

## C. Flow
- What triggers it?
- What is the main path through it?
- What state/data passes through it?

## D. Data Structures
- What objects, configuration, context, queues, AST nodes, caches, or dependency structures matter?
- How do those structures evolve during execution?

## E. Decisions
- Why is it designed in this shape?
- What tradeoffs does it embody?

## F. Practical Meaning
- What should an engineer actually remember?
- When would this knowledge matter in practice?
