# Phase 01: Motivation

## Purpose

Clarify why the idea exists, what triggered it, and which assumptions connect the trigger to a meaningful problem.

## Inputs

- `00-idea-intake.md`
- `research/00-intake-research.md`
- `sources/sources.json`

## Work

1. Classify the motivation as problem-, trend-, technology-, competitor-, strategy-, or inspiration-driven.
2. Separate the observed trigger from the proposed response.
3. Identify external changes and internal motivations.
4. State the core problem hypothesis and questions that remain open.

Suggested search questions:

- Is there a recent market, technology, product, or policy change?
- Are comparable ideas receiving sustained attention?
- Are users or practitioners discussing the underlying problem?

## Output

Write `01-motivation.md` with:

- original idea;
- motivation type and trigger;
- external background;
- core problem hypothesis;
- initial assumptions;
- clarification questions;
- Evidence Block;
- phase conclusion and downstream handoff.

Write `research/01-motivation-research.md`.

## Completion

`continue` when a plausible problem or opportunity explains the idea. Use `revise` for a materially misframed intake and `need_data` when the trigger cannot be distinguished from speculation.
