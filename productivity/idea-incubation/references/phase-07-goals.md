# Phase 07: Goals

## Purpose

Turn the validated opportunity into measurable user, business, and engineering goals.

## Inputs

- `04-painpoints.md`
- `05-value.md`
- `06-feasibility.md`
- relevant research artifacts and `sources/sources.json`

## Work

1. Define the outcome the requirement must produce.
2. Separate user, business, and engineering goals.
3. Define measurable indicators, baselines when known, and measurement methods.
4. State non-goals to prevent solution and scope drift.
5. Mark unsupported numeric targets as hypotheses.

Suggested search questions:

- Which metrics measure comparable outcomes?
- What improvement ranges are credible?
- Which activation, retention, quality, or efficiency measures fit this idea?

## Input quality gate

Require validated value, a feasible direction, and at least one observable desired outcome. Use this internal question priority for owner-defined success:

1. “成功后你最希望观察到哪个可验证结果？”
2. “目前这个结果的基线是什么？”
3. “你准备通过什么方式测量这个结果？”

Pass when the primary outcome is testable and unsupported numeric targets can be labeled as hypotheses.

## Output

Write `07-goals.md` with requirement goal, user/business/engineering goals, success metrics, measurement method, non-goals, metric rationale, Evidence Block, phase conclusion, and downstream handoff.

Write `research/07-goals-research.md`.

## Output quality gate

Verify that goals express outcomes rather than features, user/business/engineering goals are distinct, each metric has a measurement method, unknown baselines are explicit, and non-goals constrain scope.

## Completion

`continue` when goals are outcome-oriented, measurable or explicitly testable as hypotheses, and consistent with validated value and feasibility.
